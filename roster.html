Tiffany West — Theater Kid

  • Inconvenienced Millionaire
  • A good offense is a good defense

Investigate Self: Wearing high-waisted jeans, a pearl necklace, and big funky earrings. A charm bracelet with beads that spell out "Tiffany."

Descendant of Francis West. Her family has fallen on hard times due to some unfortunate legal troubles, forcing them to move back to the now-dilapidated ancestral home. Tiffany is new to town, still making friends. Her father and uncle embezzled from the family company; her father gave the feds details on her uncle to keep himself out of prison.

Stunt

Impenetrable Bluff: +2 to create advantages or defend with Deceive when using it to trick people into believing you're more capable or better positioned than you actually are.

Ashley West — Budding Zoologist

  • Plagued by Visions & Voices
  • Frustrating Little Sister

Plagued by visions and voices even before tonight, and a frustrating little sister to Tiffany the rest of the time. As a full PC in this roster, she's the one most attuned to the house's supernatural layer from the start.

Investigate Self: A field vest with a dozen pockets, worn sneakers, a smear of dirt on one knee. A well-thumbed field guide to local birds and mammals, her name written inside the cover in careful handwriting: Ashley West.

Stunts

Animal Affinity: Player picks one animal type (cats, rodents, horses, etc) at character creation, fixed for the whole game. At the start of any scene where that animal could reasonably appear, make a Lore check at Good (+3); on a success, one is present.

Berserk Rage: When you suffer a physical consequence, you can invoke it for free on your next attack. Multiple consequences = multiple free invokes.

How These Differ By Roster

Show / hide roster differences

Tiffany: 4p's Fair skills are Athletics and Provoke; 5p and 6p/7p both have Athletics and Lore at Fair instead. Average includes Stealth in 4p/5p, Fight in 6p/7p.

Corey: Trouble is theft in 5p/6p/7p, with Crafts and Will at Fair. In 4p (Kelly unused), he inherits her hit-and-run instead, with Shoot and Crafts at Fair.

Kelly: not used in 4p — her secret and Gone Hunting aspect move to Corey there.

Jennifer: build is identical in every roster; only which two people she's secretly dating changes.

Brandon: a PC in 5p/6p/7p. In 4p he's an NPC who dies during the ritual, with an expanded blackmail list. In 5p, he's a merged variant combining his own social manipulation with Joel's athleticism and gambling trouble — no boxing ring in this version, so gambling debt alone is the Aspect, avoiding stacking two Troubles on one character.

Joel: a PC in 4p/6p/7p. In 4p his Fair skills are Provoke and Stealth; in 6p/7p his Fair skills are Stealth and Fight instead, with Provoke and Shoot at Average. In 5p he doesn't exist separately — his traits transfer to Brandon instead.

Ashley: a full PC only in 7p; an NPC (Tiffany's sister) in every other roster.
